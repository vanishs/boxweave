## Scope
- One new management tool: operation_recover (23 candidate tools). Inspect is read-only. Reconcile has its own caller idempotency key, original lineage and durable receipt. Original state/event history and any identity binding, deterministic settlement, recovery audit and recovery receipt are protected by the same transaction. Actor/layer scope and authorization are checked again before commit. No native re-dispatch.
- Start/Stop/Update, Exec and ordinary Delete require original terminal proof and exact independent post-state. Unbound Create can bind a verified exact identity, without asserting original success or releasing UNKNOWN. Bound Create, File, readiness, Cancel and Exec GC support conservative diagnosis/refusal where causality cannot be proven. Network and dependent Delete stay blocked for A4; unproven claim release requires reviewed approval (A3). Existing root-reviewed local challenge/release is preserved.
- CLI diagnose/reconcile share the same service; local reviewer namespace is not a bearer credential. No schema migration, daemon, global host mutation or live deployment.
- Same-key replay returns the recorded recovery result; no fresh observation or duplicate settlement. Changed intent conflicts. Independent callers cannot settle stale snapshots; foreign layer and fabricated approval are denied.
