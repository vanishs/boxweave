# Optional task entry

[AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) contains the repository's stable boundaries. This page adds no separate rules or required reading.

1. Identify the requested outcome, target and authority. For ongoing effects, recover original receipts rather than replay.
2. Use the smallest applicable route: inspect source, change, verify, report. The [asset index](INDEX.md) and [toolbox](README.md) are optional lookup aids.
3. Stop at completion or a concrete blocker. For interrupted material work, preserve exact IDs, evidence and next safe action. Do not restart this entry or traverse its links recursively.

The Solve Loop means the requested work only. The Evolve Loop is a separate, explicitly authorized improvement task, never a condition of ordinary task completion. Pending CI or native work is not PASS; report it and avoid repeated polling without new evidence.
